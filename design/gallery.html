Daur

Personal fitness app · 400 m track concept · every screen

Gym tracking (new)

Every exercise shows its plan as sets × reps · weight. Tap an exercise to log it set by set. A rest timer runs between sets. The treadmill is timed live, and every 400 m counts as a lap.

Food and the day

Each meal is a 100 m leg of the day's lap. Logging a meal moves the runner.

Food guide (new)

Everything from your current app's Foods tab and the 10 non-negotiables, in the track's style. Allowed foods appear as chips, and "sometimes" foods have dashed edges. The keep-rare list is shown as hurdles, and the junk-food rule counts down to month 2.
